EXAMPLES (examples/)
================================================================================

examples/example_sim_basic.do
  - Basic simulation example
  - 1000 regions × 10 sectors
  - True effect known (β = 0.5)
  - Demonstrates coefficient recovery
  - OLS vs IV comparison
  - LOO robustness check
  - Status: Complete (~180 lines)

examples/example_immigration.do
  - Immigration & labor markets (Card 2009 style)
  - 250 cities × 15 origin countries
  - Historical settlement patterns as shares
  - National inflows as shocks
  - Multiple outcomes (wages, employment)
  - Alternative share robustness
  - Status: Complete (~280 lines)

examples/example_industry_growth.do
  - Trade shocks & regional growth (ADH 2013 style)
  - 180 commuting zones × 20 industries
  - Industry employment shares
  - Import competition shocks
  - Effects on unemployment, wages, population
  - Heterogeneity by education
  - Status: Complete (~320 lines)

tests/test_inference.do

tests/test_altshares.do

  - Status: Complete (~150 lines)
